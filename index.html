About MAK Overseas

International Spare Parts Supply With Local Support

MAK Overseas is a heavy machinery spare parts supplier serving construction, transport, industrial and equipment maintenance customers across India, the Gulf and export markets.

We support CAT, JCB, Komatsu, Volvo, Cummins, Hyundai, Doosan, Scania, trucks, forklifts and buses with genuine and OE-compatible parts sourced for fitment, reliability and commercial value.
