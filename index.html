About MAK Overseas

Manufactured by MAK Overseas

MAK Overseas is based in Ludhiana, India and focuses on manufacturing and export supply of heavy machinery, construction equipment and commercial vehicle spare parts for B2B customers.

The website is built for importers, distributors, dealers, workshops, fleet operators and aftermarket businesses that buy by part number, sample, drawing or bulk requirement.
